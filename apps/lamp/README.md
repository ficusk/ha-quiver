# Lamp

A dead-simple remote for one light: tap the big button to turn it on or off,
drag to dim. If your home has more than one light, pick which one Lamp drives.

Lamp doesn't connect to your home on its own. It rides on the **Home
Assistant** app: add Home Assistant to this channel and pair your home first,
then Lamp can see your lights and switch them. No Home Assistant here yet? Lamp
will tell you, and there's nothing to set up on its side.
